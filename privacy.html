Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Overhere is a beta app for doing things in your city with other people: you post a plan you're already making, and choose who joins. This page explains what we collect, who can see it, who helps us run the app, and how to have your data deleted.

The short version

  • We collect what the app needs: your account, your profile, the plans you post or join, and your group chats.
  • Other people see your name, age and profile. They never see your email, phone number, date of birth or trusted contact.
  • Your profile photo and your location never leave your phone.
  • The face check is optional. If you take it, the video goes to Amazon to confirm you're a real person; we keep only the result, never the video or images.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use advertising trackers.
  • Delete your account and data at any time: Profile → Delete my account in the app, or email admin@overhere.social.

1. Who we are

Overhere is run by a small beta team. We decide what data the app collects and why. For questions about your data, to correct or delete it, or to make a complaint, email us at admin@overhere.social.

4. Face check (and a later ID check)

The face check is optional. You can skip it at sign-up and still browse plans. You need it to post a plan, ask to join one, or see plans limited to some groups. We ask for your consent separately, at the moment you take the check. It works like this:

  • A few seconds of camera video go straight from your browser to Amazon Web Services (Amazon Rekognition Face Liveness), which confirms a real, live person is in front of the camera. The video doesn't pass through our servers, and we don't receive or keep any images.
  • We keep only the outcome: passed or failed, a confidence score, and the time.
  • After 5 attempts without passing, a person on our team reviews the outcomes and you get a notification.

Other people see only a "Verified" badge, never your check details.

Plans limited to some groups (for example women-only) are shown to people who have passed the face check and whose profile gender is in that group. The face check doesn't confirm gender: it is what you enter in your profile. Hosts see who asks to join and decide who comes.

An ID check is not available yet (it shows as "Coming soon" in the app). When it comes, it will have its own notice and consent. We will never store your full Aadhaar number, and Aadhaar will never be the only way to verify.

6. What stays on your device

The app keeps a few things in your browser's storage: your sign-in session, your light or dark theme choice, your profile photo, a few display choices (such as tips you've closed), and a copy of the app so it opens quickly and works offline. We don't use advertising cookies. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it and signs you out.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you have an account. When your account is deleted (from Profile → Delete my account, or by us when you email):

  • Upcoming plans you host are cancelled and their members are told; you leave the groups you're in and their hosts are told.
  • Your account, profile, plans, join requests, notifications, ratings, face check results and usage records are deleted.
  • Messages you sent in group chats stay, so the conversation still makes sense for the others, but they no longer show your name.
  • Feedback you sent is kept without any link to you. On request, we also delete it and your earlier beta sign-up.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data: most of it is in Profile in the app, where you can change it. For anything else, email us.
  • Delete your account and data: in the app, Profile → Delete my account. It happens straight away. You can also email admin@overhere.social from the address or phone number on your account.
  • Withdraw your consent: delete your account as above. The app can't work without your profile.
  • Control who you deal with: mute chats, leave plans, block people and report messages from inside the app.
  • Complaints: email us first and we'll try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the data protection authority where you live. In India, that is the Data Protection Board of India.

9. Age

Overhere is only for people aged 18 or over. If you believe someone younger has an account, tell us and we'll delete it.

10. Security

Everything travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). The database shows each person only what they are allowed to see, and it checks every request itself; the app in your browser can't get around those checks. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. Changes and contact

When we change this policy we update the date at the top. If a change affects how your data is used, we'll tell you in the app first.

Questions, requests or complaints: admin@overhere.social.
