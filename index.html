Questions

Is this a dating app?

No. Overhere is for going out in a small group: a movie, a coffee, a concert. Each plan has the host plus up to 10 people.

How do you keep it safe?

Everyone does a live face check before they can post or join a plan. The host approves each person, plans happen in public places, and every group chat has an SOS button. You can block or report anyone, and a person on our team reads every report.

Can I host my own plan?

Yes. Pick a movie, café or concert, set the time and place, choose how many people can come, and decide who pays. Then you approve the requests that come in.

Who pays for a plan?

The host picks one: split it equally, everyone pays their own way, or the host covers everyone.

Can a plan be just for women?

Yes. A host can limit a plan to women, men or non-binary people. Only people in that group can see the plan and ask to join.

Where does it work?

Anywhere in India. You see plans within 50 km of your home area, and you can also look at plans in other cities, such as Delhi NCR, Mumbai and Bengaluru. You can change your home area any time.

Who can see my phone number and email?

No one else on Overhere. Other people see your name, age, profile and whether you show up.

What if someone doesn't show up?

After each plan, the host marks who came. No-shows go on that person's record, so hosts can see who's reliable. If you're marked by mistake, you can tell us.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Overhere works in your phone's browser, and you can add it to your home screen. Android and iPhone apps are coming soon.
